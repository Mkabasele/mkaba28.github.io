Who I Am

I am a marketing and communications professional based in Seattle, Washington. I hold a B.S. in Business Management & Communication from Western Governors University (2025), I am HubSpot-certified, and I recently earned a certification in Strategic Thinking and Innovation.

What I Do Now

As Global Advocate for AfyaKili, a mental health organization in the DR Congo, I lead international outreach: I rebuilt its bilingual website, research and contact funders, and write partnership materials in English and French. I also coordinate fundraising galas for the New Young Scholars nonprofit.

What I Bring

Experience across marketing, operations and community leadership: I grew social media engagement by 30%, cut operational waste by 20% as a store and restaurant manager, and organized 50+ community events in Ghana. I speak English, French and Lingala, which helps me work with teams and audiences across cultures.
